Emu War Veterans

We are a group of like-minded individuals from Australia, New Zealand, South East Asia, and beyond, united by a love of social gaming without the hardcore sweat. We pride ourselves on offering an inclusive community where we want members to feel like part of a family.

Our journey began in 2022 when we formed a Hell Let Loose team for the BigD Premier League (BPL). After realizing our shared passion for playing as a team, we established EWV as a gaming group that spans across multiple games, including Hell Let Loose, War Thunder, Call of Duty, Survival Co-op games and more.

After giving the competitive Hell Let Loose scene a go, we eventually found our place as more of a casual social group, where banter, camaraderie, and fun take precedence over competition.
