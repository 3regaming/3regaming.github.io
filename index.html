THE ALLIANCE

Three clans, one roof

Each clan keeps its own identity. Membership gets you access across all three.

3PARA Gaming

3PARA Gaming is a spirited community passionate about games like Hell Let Loose, where teamwork and communication are key.

Drawing inspiration from Airborne Kiwi's history with the 3rd Battalion, The Parachute Regiment, our group includes both military veterans and avid gamers.

We embody the regiment's ethos, 'Utrinque Paratus' ('Ready for Anything'), focusing on courage and tenacity in our playstyle.

Whether you're a seasoned veteran or new to gaming, our welcoming community embraces those ready to dive into a gaming experience marked by resilience and teamwork.

AUS Renegades

We’re a group of former competitive players who have decided to shift our focus towards building a more relaxed, friendship-based gaming community.

Our goal is to create a place where members can jump into Discord, find groups of friends, and play whatever games they enjoy. Alongside our regular gaming, we also host weekly social nights, where the community comes together to play a selected game and spend some time together.

Our current games include Hell Let Loose, Battlefield 6, War Thunder, Chivalry 2, Squad 44, Gates of Hell, and Sand Raiders of Sophie. We’re also looking to bring FIFA into the Australian PC gaming community.

We’re always looking to expand the games and activities available to our members, and we’re especially looking to expand our membership base around Hell Let Loose: Vietnam.

At the end of the day, we’re about good games, good people, and building a community where everyone can jump in and have a good time.

Emu War Veterans

We are a group of like-minded individuals from Australia, New Zealand, South East Asia, and beyond, united by a love of social gaming without the hardcore sweat. We pride ourselves on offering an inclusive community where we want members to feel like part of a family.

Our journey began in 2022 when we formed a Hell Let Loose team for the BigD Premier League (BPL). After realizing our shared passion for playing as a team, we established EWV as a gaming group that spans across multiple games, including Hell Let Loose, War Thunder, Call of Duty, Survival Co-op games and more.

After giving the competitive Hell Let Loose scene a go, we eventually found our place as more of a casual social group, where banter, camaraderie, and fun take precedence over competition.
